Nitrous Oxide: Closed Air Spaces and Diffusion Hypoxia

Nitrous oxide is 34 times more soluble than nitrogen. It enters gas-filled spaces much faster than nitrogen can leave. Expandable spaces grow; rigid spaces build pressure. At emergence, nitrous floods back into the alveoli.
